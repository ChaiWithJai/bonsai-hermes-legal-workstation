# Aster Clinical AI master services agreement

FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT. For the scenario only, the parties have completed their internal signing process. No real company or person has signed this document.

Parties: A+ Active Services and Aster Clinical AI, a fictional clinical AI startup.
Effective date: September 15, 2026. Initial term: September 15 to December 15, 2026.
Order forms specify the purchased services, fees, delivery dates and named contacts. If a later signed amendment expressly replaces a term, the later text governs the affected work.

## Section 4.2, participant information and summaries

A+ Active will provide an anonymized themes summary by September 30, 2026. The summary must not include employee attribution, patient information or raw interview transcripts. Aster's designated reviewer must approve the summary before wider circulation.

## Section 4.4, original recording retention term

A+ Active will delete workshop recordings within 30 calendar days after the workshop and retain confirmation of deletion. The order form amendment dated September 24 replaces this period for recordings made September 22.

## Section 7.1, escalation

A+ Active will name a primary and backup contact for any confidentiality concern. A person, rather than the agent, decides whether a concern needs immediate legal review.

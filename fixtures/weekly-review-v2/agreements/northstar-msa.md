# Northstar Clinic Network master services agreement

FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT. Northstar is an invented clinic network, not Mayo Clinic or a real customer. The signing state is a scenario assumption, and no real signature is represented.

Parties: A+ Active Services and Northstar Clinic Network. Effective date: September 1, 2026. The agreement covers organizational facilitation and staff communication, not patient care, clinical advice or processing patient records.

## Section 3.3, site coordination

A+ Active will identify a site lead and backup for each participating clinic before on-site sessions start. Northstar controls access to its premises and approves the final participant schedule.

## Section 5.2, quarterly review

A+ Active will deliver a quarterly program review by October 9, 2026. The review will distinguish completed sessions, open concerns and proposed next actions. It must not claim clinical efficacy.

## Section 8.1, restricted information

Workshop notes must exclude patient identifiers. Any suspected disclosure is referred to Northstar's designated privacy contact and A+ Active's human lead; the agent must not close the incident.
